About

I work where code, circuits, and number theory overlap. I build custom chips for the Wokwi simulator, plan search algorithms, design rescue robots, and study the 3n+1 problem with automata and Diophantine methods.

I care about two things in everything I make: that it is well judged, and that it holds up under scrutiny.
